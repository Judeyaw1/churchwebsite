# Church Website Design Guidelines

## Design Approach
**Reference-Based Approach**: Drawing inspiration from modern spiritual and community-focused websites like Hillsong, Life.Church, and Elevation Church. The design emphasizes warmth, accessibility, and community connection while maintaining professional credibility.

## Core Design Elements

### Color Palette
**Primary Colors:**
- Deep navy blue: 220 85% 15% (trust, stability)
- Warm white: 45 20% 98% (purity, peace)
- Soft cream: 45 25% 95% (warmth, comfort)

**Accent Colors:**
- Golden amber: 40 75% 65% (hope, divine light) - used sparingly for CTAs
- Sage green: 120 25% 70% (growth, renewal) - for secondary elements

**Dark Mode:**
- Charcoal: 220 15% 12%
- Dark blue: 220 30% 8%
- Muted gold: 40 35% 45%

### Typography
**Primary:** Inter (Google Fonts) - clean, readable for body text
**Display:** Playfair Display (Google Fonts) - elegant serif for headings and scripture quotes
**Sizes:** Use consistent scale with text-sm, text-base, text-lg, text-xl, text-2xl, text-3xl

### Layout System
**Spacing Units:** Tailwind units of 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24 for consistent rhythm
**Grid:** 12-column responsive grid with generous whitespace
**Containers:** Max-width constraints for optimal reading lengths

### Component Library
**Navigation:** Clean horizontal nav with smooth mobile hamburger transition
**Cards:** Soft shadows with rounded corners for events and staff profiles
**Buttons:** Primary (solid amber), Secondary (outline with blurred background on images)
**Forms:** Gentle focus states with subtle animations
**Hero:** Large, impactful section with overlay text and video/image background

### Visual Treatments
**Gradients:** Subtle navy-to-charcoal gradients for backgrounds
**Overlays:** Semi-transparent dark overlays (opacity-60) on hero images
**Shadows:** Soft, natural drop shadows for depth
**Borders:** Minimal use, prefer subtle dividers

## Page Structure (5 Sections Maximum)

1. **Hero Section** - Welcoming message with service times CTA
2. **About/Mission** - Church vision and community focus
3. **Services & Events** - Schedule and upcoming activities
4. **Staff/Leadership** - Pastor and key team members
5. **Contact/Visit** - Location, contact info, and connection opportunities

## Images
**Hero Image:** Large, warm image of congregation or church exterior with soft lighting
**Staff Photos:** Professional headshots with consistent lighting and backgrounds
**Community Images:** Candid photos of church activities, fellowship, and service
**Background Elements:** Subtle architectural details or nature imagery

## Animations
**Entry Animations:** Gentle fade-in and slide-up transitions for content sections
**Hover States:** Subtle scale and shadow changes for interactive elements
**Navigation:** Smooth slide transitions for mobile menu
**Scroll Reveals:** Progressive disclosure of content as user scrolls
**Page Transitions:** Smooth fade between different pages/sections

Keep animations purposeful and never distracting from the spiritual message and community focus.
